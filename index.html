Multidisciplinary Approach

Leveraging Extensive Experience with an Arsenal of Knowledge

LEX GLOBAL ADVOCACY's practice areas span a broad range of industries and businesses — locally, regionally, and nationally. From high-stakes international arbitration to Indian corporate, criminal, family, taxation and property law, our integrated teams deliver end-to-end representation under a single retainer.

Through our multidisciplinary approach, we provide value to our clients by pulling from our deep knowledge base. We are determined to meet our clients' objectives in a timely and cost-effective manner on an array of issues.

International & Cross-Border Multi-jurisdictional disputes, treaty arbitrations, and transnational transactions coordinated across common-law and civil-law systems.
Corporate & M&A Company formation, joint ventures, share purchase agreements, SEBI compliance, ROC filings, private equity, and venture funding rounds.
Criminal & White Collar Bail, trial defence, economic offences, PMLA, CBI, ED, and quashing petitions before High Courts and the Supreme Court of India.
Family & Personal Law Divorce, maintenance, child custody, succession, testamentary matters, adoption, and mediation-led matrimonial settlements.
Labour & Employment Industrial disputes, POSH compliance, executive service contracts, non-compete enforcement, and labour court representation.
Tax & Regulatory Direct tax advisory, GST litigation, transfer pricing, income tax appellate tribunal appeals, and regulatory compliance.
Real Estate & Property Title diligence, RERA complaints, joint development agreements, conveyancing, and landlord–tenant disputes.
Intellectual Property & Tech Trademark, copyright, patent prosecution, IP licensing, trade secret protection, and technology transfer agreements.
Cyber & Data Protection Data breach response, DPDP Act 2023 compliance, IT Act offences, intermediary rules, and cross-border data transfer frameworks.
Constitutional & Administrative Writ petitions, fundamental rights, PILs, statutory appeals, and administrative tribunal representation.

LEX GLOBAL ADVOCACY has been proud to represent its clients with professionalism, integrity, and zealousness. The firm continues its tradition of providing a complete and diverse offering of legal services across all major Indian and international practice areas.
